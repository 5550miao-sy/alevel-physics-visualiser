Electric current

把电流想象成“电荷通过一个截面的速率”。改变导线和电子的参数，看看电流如何响应。

核心定义I = ΔQ / Δt，其中 1 ampere = 1 coulomb per second。

What is actually moving?

金属中的自由电子持续做无规则运动。接通电源后，它们在无规则运动之上产生很小的整体漂移，从而形成电流。

Electron drift←→Conventional current

Reading the equation

n 是每立方米的载流子数量，A 是导线截面积，v 是平均漂移速度，q 是单个载流子的电荷。任何一项增大，电流都会增大。

COMMON MISCONCEPTION · 常见误区

电子漂移很慢，但灯泡几乎立即亮起。原因不是某个电子瞬间从开关跑到灯泡，而是电路中的电场迅速建立，使整条导线内的电子几乎同时开始漂移。

Core relationshipv = fλ and T = 1/f
Variables & units · v = fλ and T = 1/f
v wave speed, measured in m s⁻¹
f frequency, measured in hertz (Hz)
λ wavelength, measured in metres (m)
T period, measured in seconds (s)
Travelling wave lab
调节振幅、波长和频率，观察波形及传播速度
Amplitude A2.0 cm
Wavelength λ4.0 m
Frequency f1.5 Hz
Wave speed v6.0 m s⁻¹
v = fλ = 1.5 × 4.0 = 6.0 m s⁻¹
Amplitude

The maximum displacement of a point on the wave from its equilibrium position.

Wavelength

The shortest distance between two points that are in phase.

Frequency

The number of complete oscillations passing a point per unit time.

EXAM LANGUAGE

Particles oscillate about fixed equilibrium positions; they do not travel with the wave. The wave transfers energy without a net transfer of matter.

UNIT 2 · TOPIC 3A.2

Wave types

比较介质中粒子的振动方向与能量传播方向，区分 transverse waves 和 longitudinal waves。

Classification rule Compare the direction of oscillation with the direction of energy transfer.
Particle motion lab
切换波的类型，追踪橙色粒子如何围绕平衡位置振动
Wave typeTransverse
oscillation ⟂ energy transfer
Transverse wave

The oscillations are perpendicular to the direction of energy transfer. Examples: electromagnetic waves and waves on a stretched string.

Longitudinal wave

The oscillations are parallel to the direction of energy transfer. Examples: sound waves in air.

Compression and rarefaction

A compression is a region of higher particle density; a rarefaction is a region of lower particle density.

COMMON MISCONCEPTION

The drawn sinusoidal shape of a longitudinal wave is often a graph of displacement or pressure. It does not mean that particles move up and down.

Wave phase and superposition

调节两个同频波之间的 phase difference，观察每一点的 displacement 如何相加形成 resultant wave。

Principle of superposition When waves meet, the resultant displacement at any point is the vector sum of the individual displacements.
Superposition lab
蓝色 Wave 1 + 橙色 Wave 2 = 绿色 resultant
Phase difference φ0°
φ = 360° × Δx / λ
Constructive interference
Variables & units · φ = 360° × Δx/λ
φ phase difference, measured in degrees (°)
Δx path difference, measured in metres (m)
λ wavelength, measured in metres (m)
Δx and λ must use the same length unit.
In phase

Phase difference = 0° or a whole-number multiple of 360°. Crests align with crests.

Antiphase

Phase difference = 180°. A crest aligns with a trough; equal amplitudes cancel completely.

Path and phase difference

A path difference of λ gives 360°; λ/2 gives 180°; λ/4 gives 90°.

EXAM LANGUAGE

Superposition does not permanently change the individual waves. After overlapping, each wave continues with its original shape and direction.

Stationary waves

观察两个同频、等振幅、反向传播的波如何形成固定的 nodes 和 antinodes，并比较不同 harmonic。

Formation A stationary wave is formed by the superposition of two progressive waves of the same frequency and similar amplitude travelling in opposite directions.
Stationary wave lab · fixed string
选择 harmonic；蓝点是 nodes，绿点是 antinodes
Harmonic n1
String length L1.20 m
Wavelength λ2.40 m
Frequency f50.0 Hz
Mode on a string fixed at both endsFundamental

fn = nv/(2L)
Variables & units · L = nλ/2 and fₙ = nv/(2L)
L length of the vibrating string, measured in metres (m)
n harmonic number; a positive integer with no unit
λ wavelength, measured in metres (m)
fₙ frequency of the nth harmonic, measured in hertz (Hz)
v wave speed on the string, measured in m s⁻¹
Node

A point on a stationary wave that always has zero displacement.

Antinode

A point on a stationary wave where the amplitude is a maximum.

Spacing

Adjacent nodes are separated by λ/2. A node and the nearest antinode are separated by λ/4.

COMMON MISCONCEPTION

There is no net transfer of energy along a stationary wave. All particles between two adjacent nodes oscillate in phase; particles in neighbouring loops oscillate in antiphase.

Current–voltage relationships

拖动 I–V 图上的工作点，把宏观曲线与载流子、晶格振动、碰撞和半导体势垒直接联系起来。

How to read the graph Current is plotted on the y-axis and potential difference on the x-axis. The gradient ΔI/ΔV is the conductance, so a steeper gradient means a smaller resistance.
Variables & units · V = IR, R = V/I, P = IV
V potential difference, measured in volts (V)
I current, measured in amperes (A)
R resistance, measured in ohms (Ω)
P electrical power, measured in watts (W)
ΔI/ΔV gradient of this I–V graph, equal to conductance 1/R
I–V characteristicDrag the blue operating point
Metal latticeconstant temperature
Potential difference0.00 V
Current0.00 A
Resistance V/I—
Temperature293 K
Ohmic conductor · constant temperature

Increasing potential difference increases the electric field and mean drift velocity. At constant temperature the lattice vibration and resistance remain approximately constant, so current is directly proportional to potential difference.

Ohmic conductor

Straight line through the origin. At constant temperature, resistance is constant. The mean time between scattering events is approximately unchanged while drift velocity increases.

Filament lamp

Decreasing gradient at larger |V|. Heating makes the positive metal ions vibrate more, increasing electron–lattice collisions and resistance.

Semiconductor diode

Current mainly in one direction. Forward bias reduces the junction barrier; reverse bias widens the depletion region. The relevant carriers are electrons and holes—not free electrons colliding with metal ions.

NTC thermistor

Increasing gradient as self-heating increases. A hotter semiconductor has more mobile charge carriers, so resistance falls. Increased carrier density dominates over increased lattice scattering.

EXAM PRECISION

For an I–V graph, resistance is not the gradient: gradient = ΔI/ΔV = 1/R. For a V–I graph the gradient would be resistance. Always check which quantity is on each axis.
